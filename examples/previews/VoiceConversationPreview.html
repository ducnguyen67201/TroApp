01 Name the activity

“Trò chuyện” replaces “Không gian làm việc”. “Lịch sử trò chuyện” describes past exchanges, including both speech and typing.

02 Clarify the conversation panel

Show what Tro heard and whether it is listening, processing or preparing a response. Keep the existing cursor-following HUD; this proposal adds no desktop overlay.

03 Separate receipt from success

Show what Tro heard, then what happened. A failed task keeps its context and a clear next step. History and live text here are proposed UI.
